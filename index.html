How to play

  • Blue cars start on the left edge and drive east. They can also steer north or south, never west.
  • Red cars start on the bottom edge and drive north. They can also steer west or east, never south.
  • Each turn, move one of your cars one square onto an empty square. No jumping, no capturing.
  • A car can only leave the board by driving forward off the far side (blue off the east edge, red off the north edge).
  • Win by getting both your cars off the board first.
  • Lose if it is your turn and you have no legal move. So blocking your rival in is a winning trick, and getting boxed in is a loss.
  • You always drive blue and the CPU always drives red. On the title screen you choose who moves first.

Controls: tap a car, then tap a flashing square. On a keyboard, press an arrow key to drive the selected car. House rule: if the same position appears three times, the game is a draw.
